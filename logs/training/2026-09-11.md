## What this session changed

**The chest press was under-loaded by 40 %.** It sat at 25 per side in the log since 5 Sep.
Today: 25×12 ("light"), then 35×10, then 45×4 clean. Estimated max is now about 51 per side.
Working load 40, target 4 × 6-8. This is the single biggest correction in the log so far.

**Best pull-up session yet, and it came second in the order.** +10×8 beats the previous best of
+10×6, which was set fresh as the first exercise on 8 Sep. Second set 6 reps against 4.5 on Tuesday.
Start at +12.5 kg next Upper day.

**The shoulder press machine replaces the seated dumbbell press on Upper.** His request, and the
right call: at 194 cm the 15 kg dumbbells were never the limiter, the stabilisers were.
30×7.5 today against 25×9 on Monday, a jump from about 32 to 37 kg estimated max.

**Lateral raises are a technique problem, not a load problem.** He took 15 kg (prescribed 10),
swung both sets, said so himself, then swung 8 kg as well. Standing lateral raises give him a way
to cheat and he takes it every time. Fixed by changing the setup, not the cue: seated lateral
raise from next session, back upright, hip drive physically impossible. Start 8 kg.

**Triceps pushdown deliberately cut.** Triceps were already at 17 hard sets for the week before
this session and the chest and shoulder pressing added 7 more. The weekly ceiling is 16.

**Assisted reps three times in a row.** A training partner was there and he used forced reps at
the end of the chest press, the row and the shoulder press. Only clean reps are logged. Rule given:
assisted reps on the last set of an exercise only.
