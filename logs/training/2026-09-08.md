## Coach note
The pull-up jump is real: +10 kg for 6 clean reps, done first in the session while fresh. That is
the value of exercise order, and it is exactly why the shoulder press moved up on Push day.
Second thing worth more than the numbers: he stopped the T-bar row at 35 kg because he felt himself
swinging, and dropped to 30 for strict reps. That judgement is what makes the log trustworthy.
Two loads in the written plan were far too light (hammer curl 10 kg vs 17.5 used, cable row start).
Both corrected in the program.
