## Coach note
Chest volume was the whole session: 4 + 3 + 3 + 1 sets. Dips at bodyweight are a real strength
test at this bodyweight and belong in the program, but only for 2 sets. The chest press was added
because bodyweight dips cannot be loaded down; it stays as exercise 3 from now on.
23 work sets: 13 for chest, 6 for shoulders, 3 for triceps, nothing skipped. Chest volume in one
session is too high - 13 sets is roughly double what one session can use. That is why the plan now
puts the shoulder press second: the fresh sets belong on the weak point, not on the fourth chest
machine. Drop sets on the shoulder press are fine occasionally but they make the load record
useless, so straight sets from now on. Lateral raises came in at exactly 12 reps three times, which
means 7 kg is not the limiter yet - push the reps.
RIR still not being reported — that is the one piece of data missing from every session so far.
