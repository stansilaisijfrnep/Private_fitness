## Block 1: "Feed and load"
- Start date: 2026-09-05 (Saturday, session 1 Upper).
- Length: 7 weeks. 6 training weeks plus deload in week 7. Week 1 = 5 to 11 Sep.
- Split: Upper / Lower A / Push / Pull / Legs B. Three upper days, two lower days.
- Schedule: rolling order. Training days are Monday to Friday, five per week. The next session is
  always the next one in the order after the last session logged. Sat and Sun off or sport.
  Sport goes on off days or after lifting, never before a leg day if avoidable.
  The dashboard calendar computes this automatically from the logged sessions.
- Deload rule: week 7 is a deload (same exercises, 2 sets each, 3 RIR). Earlier deload if two
  consecutive sessions regress with good sleep and food.
- Missed day rule: the order shifts forward, nothing is dropped. If a week only fits 4 sessions,
  the fifth simply lands on the next Monday. Never skip a leg day out of the order.

## Rules that apply to every session
- Warm-up: 5 minutes bike or rower, then ramp-up sets on the first exercise: empty bar or
  light x 10, 50 % x 6, 75 % x 3, then work sets. Leg days add: 2 x 10 bodyweight squats,
  10 per side lateral band walks, 5 per side hip airplanes.
- Effort: work sets end 1 to 3 reps from failure (RIR). Last set of isolation and machine work
  can go to 0 RIR. Barbell squat and trap bar deadlift stay at 3 RIR for all of block 1.
- Progression (double progression): when every set hits the top of the rep range at the target RIR,
  add load next time. Upper barbell +2.5 kg, dumbbells next pair, lower body and machines +5 kg.
  Squat and trap bar: +2.5 kg and +5 kg every session while technique holds, regardless of reps.
- Rest: 2 to 3 minutes on compounds, 60 to 90 seconds on isolation. Superset isolation pairs
  to keep sessions inside 75 minutes.
- Log every set: load, reps, RIR. A set that is not logged did not happen.
- Technique: full range of motion. A rep that shortens to move more weight does not count.
- Pain: sharp pain, stop the exercise, swap for the alternative listed, report it in the log.
- Film squat and trap bar deadlift from the side once a week in week 1 to 3. Describe or
  send what you see: bar path, depth, back position.
