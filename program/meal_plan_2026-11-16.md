## The shake (mixer, makes 2 halves, ~870 kcal, 48 g protein)
400 ml whole milk · 60 g oats · 1 banana · 30 g whey · 1 tbsp peanut butter (16 g, not more) ·
1 tbsp honey. Blend 30 s. Half in the morning, half after training (keeps 24 h in the fridge).
