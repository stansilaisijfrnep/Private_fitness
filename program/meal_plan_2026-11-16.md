# Meal plan, week of Mon 16 to Sun 22 Nov 2026 (Lidl France version)

Asked for on 16 Nov ("healthy, gain muscle and weight, I have a mixer"), then "structured, exact
shopping list for Lidl France". Targets from PROFILE: 3300 kcal, 170 g protein, 80 g fat, 475 g carbs.
Built on his rules: solid meals 350 to 650 kcal (stomach ceiling), extra calories liquid (shake, juice),
carbs front-loaded, 3 eggs max, 1 tbsp peanut butter.

Lidl France has no permanent whey, so the shake gets its protein from skyr. Ham sandwich replaces the
Hipro drinking yogurt before training. Day totals: ~3280 kcal, ~180 g P, ~455 g C, ~71 g F.

## Fixed recipes
- **Shake (mixer, 2 halves):** 500 ml whole milk + 60 g oats + 1 banana + 200 g skyr + 1 tbsp peanut butter
  + 1 tbsp honey. ~940 kcal, 47 g P. Half at 10:30, half after training. Keeps 24 h in the fridge.
- **Overnight oats (make the night before):** 80 g oats + 200 g skyr + 1 banana + 1 tbsp honey + handful frozen berries. ~590 kcal, 30 g P.
- **Eggs breakfast:** 3 eggs + 2 slices pain de mie + 250 ml orange juice. ~580 kcal, 29 g P.
- **Lunch box (batch):** 130 g raw chicken + 60 g dry rice + 80 g frozen wok veg + sauce, + 250 ml juice. ~500 kcal, 32 g P.
- **Pre-training sandwich (16:30):** 2 slices pain de mie + 4 slices jambon blanc + 1 banana + 250 ml juice. ~605 kcal, 40 g P.
- **Dinner** (see table) + 250 ml orange juice. ~550 to 680 kcal, 32 to 48 g P.
