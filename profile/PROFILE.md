## Calorie adjustment rules
- Weigh every morning after the bathroom, before food. Only the 7-day average counts.
- Week 1 and 2 are calibration. Eat 3300, log everything, and we see what the scale does.
- Average gain under 0.2 kg per week over 2 weeks: add 200 kcal (carbs).
- Average gain over 0.5 kg per week over 2 weeks: remove 150 kcal.
- Waist up more than 1 cm in 2 weeks with weight on target: remove 150 kcal, check alcohol log.

## The hard-gainer eating rules (for someone who gets full fast and forgets to eat)
1. Fixed meal times with alarms. Hunger is not the trigger, the clock is.
2. Calorie density first: white rice, pasta, bread, olive oil, nuts, nut butter, whole milk,
   cheese, eggs, fatty fish, dried fruit. Do not fill up on salad and water before the calories are in.
3. The daily shake: 500 ml whole milk, 80 g oats, 1 banana, 30 g whey, 30 g peanut butter.
   About 950 kcal and 55 g protein. Split in two if it is too much at once.
4. Every meal has 35 to 45 g protein. Meat, fish, eggs, dairy, whey.
5. Ordering defaults: rice bowl with double meat and double rice; kebab plate with rice; sushi with
   extra rice; pasta with meat. Pizza is fine, log it.
6. Carbs 1 to 2 hours before training. Shake after training if the next meal is more than 2 hours away.
7. Night out: 2 litres of water and a protein meal before bed. Next day is a normal eating and
   training day. Never "make up" for it by skipping food.
8. Single-sitting ceiling: dense solid food (rice, pasta, meat) tops out around 300 to 350 kcal
   before feeling stuffed, even with more sitting in front of them (confirmed 2026-09-05, a 330 kcal
   tuna-rice burrito could not be finished). Plan solid meals in that range, not larger. To hit a
   bigger total, add a second sitting or a shake, do not make one plate bigger. Batch-cooked meals
   should be portioned into 300 to 350 kcal servings from the start, not split into fewer, bigger ones.
